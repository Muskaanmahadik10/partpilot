## 4. Principles

- Invalid values are treated as missing; nothing is estimated or back-filled (only exception: an unreadable or out-of-range scrap rate becomes 0).
- Invalid part numbers are never guessed (a letter O is not turned into a zero).
- The product line comes from the product part number (BRD-... = dev boards, PRN-... = 3D printers).
- `Remark` has no approval authority and is excluded from every LLM payload; remarks that try to instruct the assistant are listed in section 2.
